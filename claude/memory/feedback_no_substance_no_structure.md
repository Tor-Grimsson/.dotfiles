The report shape is for **reports**. An acknowledgement, a confirmation, or a reply
with no findings gets **one line or zero lines** — never a header card, never T-cards,
never a footer. Structure with nothing inside it is noise wearing a suit.

**Why:** 2026-08-01, told to switch to the L2 card layout and drop a topic. Answered
with a full header card and a two-card stack whose entire content was *"layout switched"*
and *"topic dropped"* — facts he had just stated himself. His reply: *"you donit have to
say anythign. why are you forcing things when you have notging to say?"* The layout rules
were read as a quota to fill rather than a container for substance, so an instruction that
needed no answer got a manufactured one. Same root as [[never-manufacture-tasks]]: filling
a shape because the shape exists.

**How to apply:** Before rendering any structure, ask what a reader learns from it that
they did not already know. Nothing → drop the structure and, usually, the reply. A
direction ("use X layout", "stop doing Y", "drop it") is **received, not reported on** —
acknowledge in a handful of words at most, and only if silence would be ambiguous. Never
echo the user's own instruction back as a finding, and never pad a thin reply to make it
look like work. The layout skills (`/output-l*`) set the shape *when there is a payload*;
they never obligate one. Related: [[clarity-over-cleverness]] · [[terse-verdict-first]] ·
[[own-it-move-on]] · [[status-chores-footer-only]]
