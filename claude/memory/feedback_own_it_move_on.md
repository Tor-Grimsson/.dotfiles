On 2026-07-28 (humpty-dumpty session) the agent built nine file edits off "sure… does that make sense?" — a confirmation checkpoint, not a green light — then answered the callout with meta-explanation of what it had "treated" the message as. User: "I don't need this fake human shit. I don't care what you treated or whatever the fuck — just own it and move on."

**Why:** validation paragraphs, numbered recaps of why he was right, and self-narrating apologies are noise that stands between him and his actual point; each one costs a turn and drains him. Anger is a valid signal, not something to manage — own it, take it, move on.

**How to apply:** when called out: ≤1 line naming the concrete act ("I applied bumps you didn't authorize"), then directly the deliverable. Ask `memory? severity? global or repo-local?` once, only if unanswered. When a message approves a direction but ends in a question, answer it and confirm scope before building anything.
