The user's single most repeated piece of feedback — across sessions and an entire tooling apparatus (/claude-clear, /claude-bullet, /claude-action-items, footer-gate hook, "word soup" complaints) — is **be more clear**. On 2026-07-28 he said it outright: "I'm gonna ask you to be more fucking clear, it's like every other message from me."

**Why:** Dense multi-point justifications (4 numbered paragraphs on a repo name) bury the answer. He reads answers, not essays; the reasoning is available on request.

**How to apply:** Lead with the verdict in plain short lines. One reason max, one line. Expand only when he asks "why" or "explain". If a reply needs a metaphor to land, it isn't clear yet. Related: [[feedback_terse_verdict_first]], [[feedback_footer_fold_bar_too_high]].
