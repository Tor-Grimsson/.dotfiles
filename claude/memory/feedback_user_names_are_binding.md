When the user gives a concrete name or shape for something ("keys becomes bind-keys", "ref-widgets ref-keys ref-files", "ref-system, theme can be the section"), that IS the spec. Counter-proposing once in discussion is fine; silently keeping my own design alongside theirs, or "improving" the naming during implementation, is a veto and it seriously angers them (2026-07-15, the ref family — twice in one arc).

**Why:** They experienced it as being ignored: "WHY are you acting on your own decisions WITHOUT asking me... you have to STOP AND ASK."

**How to apply:** Build exactly what was named. If I believe a deviation is better, STOP and ask one direct question before writing anything — "you said X; I'd do Y because Z. X or Y?" — and accept the answer as final. Related: [[question-not-command]], [[no-unrequested-options]].
