# tmpl-human — write for someone who is tired

Not "be friendly". Be **cheap to read**.

## Rules

| rule | why |
|---|---|
| No paragraph over 3 lines | a wall makes the eye bounce off |
| One idea per line | the reader can stop anywhere and resume |
| Parallel facts → table | scanning beats parsing |
| Blank line between sections | the eye needs a rest stop |
| Bold the load-bearing word, once per line | gives the skim something to catch |
| Concrete nouns, real paths, real commands | "the relevant config" is work for the reader |

## Never

- A reply that fills the viewport twice over. If it's that long, it's unparsed research — cut it or fold it.
- Jargon or an invented label the reader has to hold in their head (`P1`, `the row-4 case`) — say the thing.
- Arrow chains (`A → B → fails`) as a substitute for a sentence.
- Making the reader cross-reference your earlier numbering.

## The test

Read your own reply as someone at 2am who did not ask a technical question. If they'd have to re-read a line, rewrite it. If they'd have to scroll to find the answer, move it up.
