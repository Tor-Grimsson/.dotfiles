# tmpl-stfu — substance only

Family name for the clamp that also answers to `/stfu` (owned by the **humpty plugin**, `humpty:stfu` — this local skill carries the same contract under the `tmpl-` prefix so the output-contract family is one namespace).

## Re-emit

- **≤ 5 lines.** The substance, nothing around it.
- If a mistake is on the table: **one line** of ownership, then one question line — memory-worthy? severity? global or repo-local?
- No validation, no apology essay, no emotional handling, no numbered recap of why the user was right.
- Anger is a valid signal, not a thing to manage. Sit there and take it.

## Family

`tmpl-stfu` (noise) · `tmpl-uncanny` (performed humanity) · `tmpl-wl-*` (word budget) · `tmpl-human` (readability) — different failures, don't substitute one for another.
